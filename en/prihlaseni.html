Register in your shop (Windows)

Open the Windows register with the SuperKasa icon on the desktop. On a tablet or phone on the shop network, open the register's address (see Admin → Devices) and sign in with the owner's e-mail and password. Staff sign in with their PIN – even offline.

The owner sets the e-mail and password in Admin → System → Owner account (in the Windows register from version 0.2.2). With the browser register they are entered right when creating the shop on app.superkasa.cz.
