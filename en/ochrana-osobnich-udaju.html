The binding version is in Czech

The Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy apply in their Czech version. We do not offer a translation so that no differences arise between languages; below is a non-binding summary only.

Read the Czech version

Main points (for guidance only, not binding)

  • The website uses no cookies or tracking tools.
  • The shop's data is on the SuperKasa server in the Czech Republic, it is backed up daily and the server sends sales to EET. SuperKasa processes the data of the shop's customers and staff as a processor under the agreement in the Terms and Conditions.
  • When a product is looked up, the barcode is sent. Contributing to the shared database is on by default and can be switched off in Admin: the name, pack size, group, VAT rate and your product photo are sent too – never prices or sales.
  • Retention: an enquiry 1 year, contract data for the term of the contract and 3 years after it ends, tax documents 10 years, IP addresses at most 30 days. The shop's data in the POS is deleted 12 months after the contract ends.
  • Recipients: INTERNET CZ (server in the Czech Republic), GitHub (website, USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework), Google (e-mail), the accountant; and by law the Financial Administration.
  • You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object – use the contact details on the website. You can lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz).
