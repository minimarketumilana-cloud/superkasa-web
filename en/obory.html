Can't find your type of business? When setting up the POS, describe in your own words what you sell, and the POS will suggest groups with VAT rates, pictures and first items – free and offline, in Czech, Vietnamese or English. You'll see everything and can edit it before you create the POS. Check the VAT (DPH) rates with your accountant.

Or choose Other shop or Other services and adjust the groups to suit you. We're also happy to help by phone: +420 608 886 882.
