Get started with the POS

SuperKasa runs in the browser. Nothing to download or install, the first 10 days are free, with no card and no commitment.

  1. Create your till in the browserCreate your till in the browser – on a computer, tablet or phone.
  2. Choose your type of business and add your cashiersThe POS sets up groups, VAT rates, tiles and features. Or describe in your own words what you sell, and the POS will suggest groups with VAT rates, pictures and first items – free and offline, in Czech, Vietnamese or English. You'll see everything and can edit it; check the VAT rates with your accountant. Cashiers log in with their own PIN.
  3. Try it free for 10 daysUntil the end of 2026 the POS issues valid receipts even during the trial. From 1 January 2027, live sales with EET require a paid plan. Training mode is optional, for training staff.
  4. Switch on live EET 2.0With a paid plan and a POS certificate from DIS+, you switch on live sales reporting in Admin → EET 2.0, from 1 January 2027 at the latest. If you do not have to record sales, choose “I do not have to record sales” there. Getting started with EET 2.0

Till in the browser

Works in Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge on a Windows computer, a tablet or a phone. You create your shop in a few minutes and sign in with the owner's e-mail and password.

Create your till Call

How it works

  • No file to download, nothing to install
  • The till opens at your shop's own address
  • On other devices, just sign in with e-mail and password
  • If the internet drops, you keep selling and receipts are sent later
  • The browser prints receipts on the printer you have in Windows
  • Data is backed up every day

What you will need

A computer or tablet

A Windows computer with Chrome or Edge, ideally with a touchscreen. More tills run on a tablet or phone.

Receipt printer

A thermal printer, 80 or 58 mm roll, for example Epson TM-T88. It just needs to be installed in Windows; the till prints receipts, price labels and reports on it.

Barcode scanner

An ordinary USB scanner, or the camera of a phone or tablet.

Internet

For sending to EET 2.0 and backups. If it drops, you keep selling.

Depending on the trade

Label scales, a second monitor as a customer display, a card terminal, a tablet for table service and a kitchen ticket printer.

EET certificate

For live operation, a certificate from the Financial Administration – free on the CA EET portal via DIS+. EET 2.0 guide
