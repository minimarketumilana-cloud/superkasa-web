How you pay

Rental

CZK 149 a month or CZK 1,490 a year (2 months free), always incl. VAT. Updates, new features and support are always included. The rental is valid until the paid-up date and does not renew automatically.

Buy a licence

CZK 4,000 once, incl. VAT. The till is yours for good. It includes 2 years of new features, support and warranty. After that it keeps working without limits, just without new features and support. Legal updates (EET 2.0, VAT changes) and security fixes are always free. It pays off if you want the till for 3 years or more.

Extending updates

For a bought licence you can extend new features and support for CZK 990 a year incl. VAT, at any time, even after they end. New features unlock from the day of payment.

Everything included

The POS runs in the browser, nothing is installed. Backups and running SuperKasa Cloud are included in both rental and purchase. No commitment: if you do not pay the next rental period, the rental simply ends.

10-day free trial

A new POS runs free for 10 days. You can sell, print and set up everything you need. Until the end of 2026 the POS issues valid receipts even during the trial. Training mode is there for training staff. From 1 January 2027, live sales with EET require a paid plan.

After the trial ends, the POS shows warnings for another 3 days; then, without payment, it stops new sales. Your data stays in your shop's account.

When payment ends

Sales and EET keep running for another 3 days with a clear warning. Then new sales stop, but receipts, closings, refunds and data export keep working. Your data stays in your shop's account for 12 months after the rental ends, then it is deleted – export it in time.

Frequently asked questions

Is the price final?

Yes, CZK 149 a month and CZK 1,490 a year are prices including VAT.

Rent or buy?

Rental (CZK 149 a month, CZK 1,490 a year) always has new features and support. Buying a licence for CZK 4,000 is roughly two years of yearly rental (CZK 2,980), and the till stays yours for good. It pays off if you want the till for 3+ years and do not need new features after two years – or extend them for CZK 990 a year. A purchase is the plan without paid add-ons.

What applies two years after buying?

The till keeps working without limits and your data stays. New features and support end until you extend them (CZK 990 a year). Legal updates – EET 2.0, VAT changes and mandatory fixes – and security fixes are always free, so the till never breaks the law.

Does the price include a computer, printer or scanner?

No, the price is for the POS software. You get the computer or tablet, receipt printer, barcode scanner and, if needed, scales or a customer display yourself. We're happy to advise on what is suitable – what you will need.

How many devices can I use?

Every plan includes up to 10 POS devices – for example the till, a tablet for receiving goods and phones for staff. For larger businesses, get in touch.

What are paid add-ons and how much do they cost?

Add-ons add features beyond the plan: Attendance and shifts (CZK 79), Loyalty and gift vouchers (CZK 69), Reservations and appointments (CZK 99), Recipes and portion costing (CZK 79) and Staff control (CZK 69) – per month incl. VAT, a year costs 10 months. All are free during the 10-day trial. No commitment: you only pay for the ones you switch on, and they are unlocked by the same licence key as the plan. Add-ons overview

How do I pay for the rental?

In the POS, tap Pay in the top bar (or Admin → System → Licence and plan), choose a month, a year or buying the licence and pay with the QR code in your banking app. For now we match payments by hand – if the rental isn't extended right away, give us a call.

Does the rental renew automatically?

No. The rental is valid until the paid-up date and nothing is charged automatically. You pay the next period the same way, with a QR code in the POS. If you don't pay, the POS keeps running for another 3 days with a warning.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Your data stays in your shop's account for 12 months after the rental ends. After 3 days with a warning, new sales stop, but receipts, closings and data export keep working.
