What's ready as soon as you start

When you first start the POS, you choose the business type “Butcher's shop” and it sets up the following for you. You can change all of it later in Admin.

Product groups

  • Meat & sausages
  • Dairy
  • Groceries
  • Bakery
  • Bags

Plus the groups Uncategorised 12 % and 21 %. The VAT (DPH) rates in the groups are only a suggestion – check them with your accountant.

Tiles and sample items

Tabs at the bottom of the sales screen: Meat & sausages.

Pork leg and neck, chicken breast, beef hindquarter, minced meat, frankfurters, ham and grilling sausages by the kg – and bags.

Prices are approximate – you can change them right from the sales screen.

Features switched on

  • Stock
  • Expiry
  • Price labels
  • Scales and weighed-item barcodes
  • Promotions and quantity prices
  • Customer display
  • QR payments

Sales, EET 2.0, receipts, cash and closing always work.

What you'll find useful

Meat by the kilogram

The POS asks for the weight, or reads the barcode from a scale label with the weight or price.

Expiry

Best-before dates when receiving goods, an overview of goods that will soon expire, and a badge right on the tile.

Price labels with the price per kg

Price labels with the price per kilogram, promotional price and barcode.

Promotions

Promotional prices until a chosen date – the POS applies them itself.

Grocery plan

CZK 199 a month or CZK 2,149 a year, always incl. VAT. The first 10 days are free in training mode.

Switched on from the start

Stock
Receiving goods, stock counts, stock level on the tile, what's running low and order suggestions.
Expiry
Best-before date on the item card and when receiving goods, alerts for expired goods.
Price labels
Print price labels on the receipt printer, a label printer or A4.
Scales and weighed-item barcodes
Barcodes from scale labels – with weight or price.
Promotions and quantity prices
Promotions until a set date, a lower unit price from a certain quantity.
Customer display
A second screen for the customer showing the purchase and the QR payment.
QR payments
Bank transfer payment via a QR code on the display and on the receipt.

You can switch on at any time

Deposits
Returnable bottles and crates; the deposit can be added to beer automatically.
Tables and bills
Table bills for pubs and restaurants: adding items, splitting, moving, kitchen and bar tickets.
Tips
Tips outside sales and EET: who, how much, cash or card; a monthly overview that watches the 7 % limit for tax exemption.

Switch them on in Admin → System → Register features. They are included in the plan price.
