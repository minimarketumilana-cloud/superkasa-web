MOJE eet or a POS system: what the state app can and cannot do

MOJE eet is a free web app from Finanční správa (the Financial Administration) for registering sales, and its launch is planned for 1 December 2026. It may be enough if you only want to register a few payments. A POS system makes sense when you also need stock, printing and an overview of your business.

Which option suits whom

MOJE eet is intended mainly for small businesses with a lower number of payments and at most 2 recording units (evidenční jednotky). A POS system is more suitable when you need more than registration alone. This description is based on information published by Finanční správa (the Financial Administration, the Czech tax authority) up to 31 October 2026.

SituationSensible choice
Hairdresser without employees, a few customers a day, several types of serviceMOJE eet may be enough
Stall with a small range and a phone or tabletMOJE eet may be enough
Convenience store with hundreds of items and a barcode scannerPOS system

Small business that already has a POSFirst ask your supplier about an update

A self-employed person (OSVČ) in band 1 of the flat-rate tax has a third option, the EET OFF regime. In this regime they do not register sales and pay a surcharge.

What MOJE eet can do according to Finanční správa

The app is not running yet, so we rely on the description from Finanční správa. According to it, the app will be:

  • free,
  • available in an ordinary web browser without installation,
  • usable on devices connected to the internet, for example a tablet,
  • linked to DIS+ and to the EET certification authority,
  • able to register sales as the law requires,
  • able to send a message again automatically if needed,
  • equipped with the option of creating a catalogue of goods and services with predefined items and prices,
  • able to create a document in PDF format.

According to Finanční správa, the app arranges the certificate by itself, so you do not have to download or upload it. However, you must pair the app in DIS+. You use the PROFILY MOJE EET (MOJE eet profiles) tile for this, which appears after you create your first recording unit.

Who MOJE eet is intended for and what its limits are

The eet.gov.cz website says the app is intended especially for small businesses with a lower volume of transactions and at most 2 recording units. A presentation for developers from June 2026 also states at most 2 employees and a low volume of transactions.

The exact volume limit has not been published. It is also not clear from the available texts whether the employee limit will apply in the app or whether it can technically be exceeded. Before you build your business on the app, check the conditions directly on eet.gov.cz or on the helpline 953 109 109.

How the two options differ in a normal day

You will notice the difference mainly with a larger number of customers. The following examples are models, because MOJE eet is not running yet.

Hairdresser: she has five customers a day and eight types of service. She saves the services and prices in the catalogue. After a haircut she selects the item on a tablet, takes the payment and the sale is sent. She creates a PDF receipt if the customer asks for one. She does not deal with stock. A hairdresser like this may manage with the app.

Convenience store: it has hundreds of items and a queue before closing time. It needs to scan barcodes, work with stock, print documents quickly and check the till at the end of a shift. These things are not in the published description of MOJE eet. That is why a POS system makes sense.

Bistro: it accepts cash, cards and meal vouchers, employees take turns, and the owner wants to see sales by shift. According to the presentation for developers, the app is intended for businesses with at most 2 employees. So the bistro must check whether it falls within this group at all.

How to start with MOJE eet

  1. Log in to DIS+ on the MOJE daně (My Taxes) portal and activate EET.
  2. Create a recording unit, for example your premises.
  3. After the app launches, open the PROFILY MOJE EET (MOJE eet profiles) tile in DIS+ and link up with the app.
  4. Create a catalogue of goods and services in the app.
  5. Before 1 January 2027, try how the app works on your device.

We did not find a detailed guide to the app itself by 31 October 2026. Follow eet.gov.cz. According to the schedule, there is one month to try the app before registration begins. So have a backup plan in case it does not suit you.

What to ask your POS supplier

Finanční správa states that for a POS device from the time of the original EET, a software update is usually enough. Before you decide, ask your supplier about these things:

  • From when will the POS support EET 2.0, and how much does the update cost?
  • How do I upload the .p12 file and password to it?
  • Can I test the connection in verification mode (ověřovací mód) before live operation?
  • Will it send sales by itself after an internet outage, and will it show me if sending fails?
  • Can it handle returned goods as a negative sale?
  • Does it watch the end of the certificate's validity, and can it renew it by itself?
  • Can it issue the documents I need as a VAT payer or non-payer?

If you do not get a clear answer to these questions in time, look for another solution. MOJE eet can be a temporary option if you meet its conditions.

The law does not limit your choice of device, so you may move elsewhere later. If you move from MOJE eet to a POS, you will need a certificate for the POS. Whether data can be exported from the app is not yet documented.

What is the same for both options

  • Access to DIS+. You need it in both cases. The procedure is in the article EET 2.0 step by step.
  • Recording unit. You create it in DIS+ for both the app and the POS.
  • Internet. Finanční správa says the POS device must be connected to the internet.
  • The 48-hour deadline. After an outage you must send the sale without undue delay, at the latest within 48 hours.
  • Documents. Neither the app nor a POS cancels the duty to issue a document under zákon o DPH (the VAT Act) or zákon o ochraně spotřebitele (the Consumer Protection Act).
  • Tax credit. A business owner who is a natural person and files a tax return can claim a tax credit of up to CZK 5,000, regardless of what they use for registration. In the flat-rate regime the credit is not claimed. This is our reading of the law. Check it with the tax office (finanční úřad) or your accountant. The conditions are in the article EET 2.0: basic guide.

How to decide

  1. Count your payments. How many payments do you have a day at peak times? Try to imagine entering them on a tablet.
  2. Count your items. A few services are easy to enter. Hundreds of types of goods with barcodes need a scanner and stock management.
  3. Add up your premises and people. With more than 2 recording units, MOJE eet is not enough.
  4. Think about receipts. Is a PDF enough for you, or do you print a document for customers right at the till?
  5. Ask your accountant. What data does your accountant need from you, and in what form?
  6. Work out the costs. For the app you need a device and internet. For a POS, add the licence and any accessories.

Frequently asked questions

When will MOJE eet be available?

The Finanční správa schedule states a launch on 1 December 2026. The practical information page says the launch is expected from this date.

Is MOJE eet really free?

Yes, Finanční správa describes it as a free app. You only need your own device with internet.

Do I need a POS certificate for MOJE eet?

You do not have to download or upload it. According to Finanční správa, the app arranges it automatically after it is paired with DIS+. However, you must create a recording unit.

Will MOJE eet print a receipt?

Creating a document in PDF format is documented. Published materials do not mention printing on a receipt printer.

Does MOJE eet have stock management?

Published materials mention a catalogue of goods and services with items and prices. They say nothing about stock.

How many premises can I have in MOJE eet?

Finanční správa states at most 2 recording units. A recording unit is a premises, but also, for example, an e-shop or a mobile stall.
