Why SuperKasa

A full POS with stock and price labels at one low price – no hidden fees and no expensive hardware.

CZK 189 a month incl. VAT

The final price for any type of business, or CZK 2,041 a year. Unlimited items – stock, promotions and the customer display are included in every plan. First 10 days free. Pricing

No transaction fees

We take nothing from your sales, and you don't need a terminal from us. Card payments go through your own card terminal from any bank; customers can also pay in cash or by QR code.

Computer, tablet and phone

The main till on a computer with a touchscreen, more tills on tablets and phones – all in the browser. Up to 10 devices included in the plan, all selling into one shared shift. For example the till, a tablet for receiving goods and the owner's phone.

Works without internet too

If the internet goes down, you keep selling for cash and card. Receipts upload by themselves once you're back online and go to EET with their original time. Data is backed up every day on the server and off it.

Staff can use Vietnamese

Czech, tiếng Việt and English – each cashier has their own language and switches it with one button. The customer receipt is always in Czech.

Stock included in the plan

Receiving goods by scanning, stock counts and order suggestions in every plan; for shops also expiry dates and price labels on the receipt printer. Add attendance, a loyalty programme or reservations only if you want them. Add-ons

What's new

SuperKasa improves every week. Here is the latest – included in the plan.

Notifications on your phone

Hear about every sale on your phone straight away – amount, payment, cashier and items. Free, no extra app, and you can switch notifications off at any time. On iPhone, from the home-screen icon.

Shared product database with photos

Scan an unknown barcode and the POS fills in the name and photo from the shared SuperKasa database. You just check the name and enter the price.

Connected to E-shop super

Orders from your online shop on eshopsuper.cz arrive straight at the till, and the online shop and the till share one stock.

The till on your phone, printing at the main till

On a phone, search and the scanner are on top with the current sale right below. Receipts and price labels come out of the printer at the main till – over the internet, the phone doesn't need the same Wi-Fi.

Printer set up in a minute

The wizard walks you through it step by step: on a computer you print through the browser, and on an Android tablet or phone it connects a Bluetooth or USB printer. You print a test page right away.

Reports for your accountant

Daily sales by VAT rate and payment method, stock valuation, receipts and closings – to Excel with one tap.

Ready for 2027

From 1 January 2027, EET 2.0 (electronic sales registration) begins, and in food service the rules for tips and VAT (DPH) change. SuperKasa handles this within the plan price.

EET 2.0

The receipt is saved immediately and sent to EET in the background. If the internet goes down, the POS keeps retrying and doesn't hold up sales. Getting started with EET 2.0

Tax-free tips up to 7%

You record employee tips outside sales and EET – who gets them, how much, cash or card. The POS records tips the business keeps as sales. The POS watches the 7% limit for tax exemption and prepares a monthly summary for payroll.

12% VAT on soft drinks served on site

From 2027, soft drinks and coffee served on site are taxed at 12%, take-away at 21%. On the POS you switch between Eat in / Take away and the receipt uses the correct rate.

Check dates and conditions with the Financial Administration (Finanční správa) or your accountant. How it works in the POS

How to get started
