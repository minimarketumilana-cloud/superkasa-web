What the installer does

  • One file; the installer is in Czech and needs no administrator rights
  • You don't need to install anything else
  • Desktop shortcuts “SuperKasa” and “SuperKasa – správa” (admin)
  • Optional automatic start when the computer is switched on
  • Transfer of data from another computer running SuperKasa
  • After uninstalling, your data stays for a new installation

What you will need

A Windows computer

For the main POS, a computer with Windows 10 or 11, ideally with a touchscreen monitor. Other POS windows run in a browser – even on an older Windows 7 computer or on a tablet.

Receipt printer

A thermal paper printer, 80 or 58 mm rolls – Epson TM-T88 and compatible. The POS prints receipts and price labels on it directly and opens the cash drawer through it.

Barcode scanner

An ordinary USB scanner, or the camera of a mobile phone or tablet.

Internet

For sending to EET 2.0, updates and a second backup copy in OneDrive. If it goes down, you keep selling.

Depending on your business

Scales with labels, a second monitor as a customer display, a card terminal, a label printer for price labels, a tablet for table service and a kitchen ticket printer.

POS certificate for EET

For live operation, a certificate from the Financial Administration (Finanční správa) – free on the CA EET portal via DIS+, the tax authority's online data box. Getting started with EET 2.0
