The POS in Vietnamese for convenience stores and grocery shops

A cashier can have the POS in Vietnamese and the customer still gets a receipt in Czech. This guide shows how to set this up in a convenience store or Asian food store.

Staff language and receipt language

SuperKasa separates the cashier's language from the language of the receipt.

WhatLanguage
Buttons, menu, AdminAccording to the cashier: Czech, Vietnamese, English
Names of product groupsAccording to the cashier
Receipt and invoiceAlways Czech
Price labelsAlways Czech
Item namesAs you write them. In the POS they should be in Czech.

How to set up Vietnamese

During setup. Tap the Vietnamese flag at the top right. The flag is on the sign-up page app.superkasa.cz and on the setup screen. After sign-up you go to your shop's address, where you need to tap the flag again. The owner account is then saved in Vietnamese.

For cashiers.

  1. Open Admin → Staff.
  2. Tap a cashier, or + New staff member.
  3. In the Language field, select Tiếng Việt.
  4. Tap Save.

After PIN login, the POS switches to the cashier's language. Each person can also change the language with the flag in the top bar, and the choice is saved for them. A Czech cashier and a Vietnamese owner can thus use the same POS, each in their own language.

Cheat sheet for the wall. In Admin → Staff there is a Cheat sheet for cashiers (CZ / VI) button. It prints an overview of the most common procedures on A4, in Czech and Vietnamese.

Buttons, menus and server messages are translated into Vietnamese. If you still come across Czech text, give us a call and we will add the translation.

Product groups

During setup, choose the type of business Convenience store, minimarket (in Vietnamese Tạp hóa, siêu thị mini), or Asian food store (Thực phẩm châu Á).

  • Convenience store gets groups such as tobacco, alcohol, drinks, bakery, fruit and vegetables, deposits and bags.
  • Asian food store also gets sample items, for example coriander or tofu.

You can also describe your range in your own words in Vietnamese, for example “thực phẩm châu Á, rượu sake, gạo, rau thơm”. The POS suggests groups with VAT rates, free and without internet. You can edit the suggestion before you create the POS. Check the rates with your accountant.

The names of groups that the POS creates by itself are translated. For your own group, add a translation:

  1. Open Admin → Groups and tap the group.
  2. Fill in Vietnamese name (for staff).
  3. Save.

Mark cigarettes in the item card as Fixed price – no discounts (cigarettes). Deposits on bottles are ready under PLU 0 and 3, crates under PLU 8 and 9.

Czech on the receipt

The receipt shows the item name exactly as it is in the item card. The POS does not translate it. So write names in Czech.

  • Instead of “Bún gạo”, write “Rýžové nudle 200 g” (rice noodles 200 g).
  • Put the pack size in the name. The POS uses it to calculate the unit price.

So that a cashier can recognise goods even without Czech, add a photo. In the item card, tap Upload photo or Search the internet. The Fill in photos by EAN button in Admin → Stock → Items tries to find photos for all goods with a barcode. A tile with a picture is then quicker to find than text.

If you scan an unknown code, the POS tries to find a Czech name in the SuperKasa shared database and in Open Food Facts. You just check the name and fill in the price. If this does not work, check the option Look up names by barcode online in Admin → System → Receipt printing.

Labels on the receipt, such as CELKEM (total) or Hotově (cash), are fixed in Czech. You can edit your own text in Admin → Business in the Receipt footer field. Write it in Czech. What must be on a receipt is described in the guide What must be on a receipt.

A normal day in a convenience store

A cashier deals with these situations every day.

SituationWhat to do
A customer returns a bottleType PLU 3 and Enter. A deposit of −CZK 3 is added. A negative sale is paid out of the till.
Beer with a bottleIn the item card, set Sell together with. The deposit is then added by itself.
Bread rolls by the pieceType the number, tap × and then the tile.
Fruit by weightTap the tile. The POS asks for the weight in kg.
Label from a scaleScan it. The POS reads the PLU and the weight or price from the EAN-13 code.
New cigarette priceTap the item in the sale and change the price. This applies only to this sale. You change the price permanently in the item card. A discount on goods with a fixed price is not possible.
A customer asks about a pricePress F2 and scan the item. Nothing is sold. F2 switches the check off.

Training a new cashier

  1. Add her in Admin → Staff and set her language to Tiếng Việt.
  2. Print the Cheat sheet for cashiers (CZ / VI).
  3. Let her do a few sales with you. You cancel a sale before payment with the Cancel button above the sale.
  4. Tell her that a void or refund is confirmed by a manager or the owner with their PIN.

Training mode, in which receipts are not valid, can be switched on only as long as the POS has not issued any live receipt. The Back to training button is in Admin → System in the Live section.

Price labels

You print price labels on the receipt printer, on a label printer or on A4. They are always in Czech, even if the cashier has the POS in Vietnamese.

  1. Open Admin → Print price labels.
  2. Select the goods and the size.
  3. Leave Price per kg / l (required by law) switched on.
  4. Tap Print labels.

A thermal price label shows the name, price, unit price and barcode. The print date is not printed on it. A date appears only for a promotion, when you fill in Promo valid until. Details about the printer are in the guide Receipt printer, barcode scanner and cash drawer.

Help with EET 2.0

The EET 2.0 sales registration begins on 1 January 2027. In Admin → EET 2.0 there is a procedure in seven steps, in Vietnamese and Czech. Your accountant can help you log in to DIS+, or you can call us.

  1. In DIS+, sign up for sales registration and create your shop as a recording unit (evidenční jednotka).
  2. Generate a POS certificate (pokladní certifikát) and download the .p12 file. Save the password.
  3. In Admin → EET 2.0, upload the file and password and tap Verify connection.
  4. In 2026 you can choose Dry run mode. Switch on live EET on 1 January 2027, at the latest for the first sale in 2027.

Live EET requires a paid licence. The detailed procedure is in the guide EET 2.0 step by step. The SuperKasa website is also available in Vietnamese.

We can help you by phone, in Vietnamese too: +420 608 886 882. We will go through the setup with you.

Frequently asked questions

Will the receipt be in Vietnamese if I have the POS in Vietnamese?

No. The customer receipt is always in Czech. Only the buttons, menus and product groups for the cashier are in Vietnamese.

Can each cashier have a different language?

Yes. The language is saved for each cashier. After PIN login, the POS switches by itself.

Will the POS translate item names into Vietnamese?

No. Groups are translated, but item names stay as you write them. Because of the receipt, write them in Czech.

Is Admin in Vietnamese too?

Yes. The flag is also in Admin and the choice is saved to your account.

Will you help me with setup in Vietnamese?

Yes. Call +420 608 886 882. We will set up the POS with you.
