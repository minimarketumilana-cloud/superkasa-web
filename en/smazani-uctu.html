Delete your account and shop data

For the SuperKasa app for Android and iOS and the SuperKasa POS in the browser.

App and provider

This page covers the SuperKasa app for Android and iOS (cz.superkasa.app) and the SuperKasa POS in the browser. The account is the shop account in SuperKasa Cloud (name.superkasa.cz); the owner signs in with e-mail and password. Staff have no separate account – their profiles and PINs are part of the shop account and are deleted with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Sign in as the owner – in the SuperKasa app or in a browser at app.superkasa.cz. You do not need the app for this.
  2. Open Admin → System → Close the account and delete the shop's data.
  3. First download a data export: receipts must be kept for the period required by law, and they cannot be restored after deletion.
  4. Enter the owner password, type the shop address and confirm.
  5. A confirmation with the deletion date is sent to the account e-mail.

When

The account is deleted 30 days after the request. Until then the POS works as usual and you can cancel it in the same place (Cancel the deletion request). A confirmation is sent after deletion. Deleted data disappears from backups within a further 30 days.

What is deleted

The whole shop account with all its data: products and photos, stock, receipts and closings, staff and PINs, loyalty customers, bookings, attendance, change log, settings, the EET certificate, paired devices and notification settings.

What is kept

  • Tax documents issued by SuperKasa for the rental or licence and records of these payments – for the period required by law (10 years).
  • Contract data (name, company ID, e-mail, phone) for at most 3 years after the contract ends.
  • Product data and photos already sent to the shared product database – no prices and no personal data.

Deleting only some data

Without closing the account, the owner can delete an individual loyalty customer or switch off phone notifications in Admin. What stays on the phone: the app remembers the address of the last opened shop; the POS inside it stores the device sign-in cookie, device settings and, when the internet is down, receipts not yet sent to the server. A data export is only a temporary file for the Share menu. On iPhone and iPad the app deletes it once sharing is finished, otherwise with the next export or the next app launch; on Android at the latest with the next export or the next app launch (and within an hour while the app is running). Uninstalling the app removes all of it from the phone.

Cannot sign in?

Reset a forgotten password on the shop's sign-in screen with the account e-mail and the owner PIN. For anything else call +420 608 886 882 or write to info@superkasa.cz.
